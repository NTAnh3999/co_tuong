## Movement rules reference (same as T1.2's brief — re-derive test scenarios from these, don't just duplicate T1.2's existing cases with different squares)

- **GENERAL**: one orthogonal step, must stay inside own palace (files d-f = file index 3-5; Red ranks 0-2, Black ranks 7-9).
- **ADVISOR**: one diagonal step, must stay inside own palace.
- **ELEPHANT**: exactly two points diagonally; blocked if the diagonal midpoint ("eye") is occupied; cannot cross the river (Red stays rank ≤ 4, Black stays rank ≥ 5).
- **HORSE**: L-shape (±1,±2) or (±2,±1); blocked if the orthogonal "leg" square (one step in the direction of the longer axis) is occupied.
- **CHARIOT**: slides any distance orthogonally, stops at first occupied square (capturable if enemy, blocked if friendly).
- **CANNON**: slides to empty squares without jumping (non-capturing); captures the first enemy piece found after jumping over EXACTLY one screen piece (either color) — zero screens or two-or-more screens before the target means no capture there.
- **SOLDIER**: before crossing the river, one step forward only; after crossing, forward or sideways (never backward). Forward is toward increasing rank for Red, decreasing rank for Black.

## Suggested scenarios to reach ≥10 per piece (adapt freely — these are starting ideas, not a rigid checklist; use your judgment to also cover anything T1.2's existing tests missed)

- **GENERAL**: all 4 palace corners (each has exactly 2 legal destinations), the palace center (4 destinations), blocked by a friendly piece on each of the 4 sides independently, capturing an enemy piece on each of the 4 sides, Black's General in Black's palace (not just Red's, to make sure color-specific palace bounds work both ways).
- **ADVISOR**: all 4 corner positions (each has exactly 1 destination, the center), the center position (4 destinations), blocked-by-friendly on a corner, capture-enemy on a corner, Black's Advisor in Black's palace.
- **ELEPHANT**: all 4 diagonal directions from a central home-side square, each of the 4 possible eye-blockers tested independently (not just one as in T1.2), a position where 2 of 4 diagonals are blocked by eyes and the other 2 aren't, Black's Elephant on Black's side of the river, an Elephant at a board corner (fewer than 4 diagonal options exist at all due to board edges, independent of eye/river rules).
- **HORSE**: each of the 8 possible leg-blockers tested independently (not just one as in T1.2), a horse in a board corner (only 2 of 8 destinations are even on the board), a horse on an edge file/rank (fewer than 8 destinations purely from board bounds, separate from leg-blocking), capturing an enemy on one of the 8 destinations, blocked-by-friendly on one of the 8 destinations (distinct from leg-blocking — the destination itself occupied by a friendly piece vs. the leg occupied).
- **CHARIOT**: all 4 directions independently blocked by a friendly piece at varying distances, all 4 directions independently capturing an enemy at varying distances, a Chariot in a corner (only 2 directions have any squares at all), a Chariot completely boxed in by friendly pieces on all 4 adjacent squares (zero legal destinations).
- **CANNON**: one-screen capture in all 4 directions independently, zero-screen (can't capture, but CAN still slide to empty squares before the first piece) in multiple directions, two-screen (can't capture past the 2nd piece) in multiple directions, a screen that is the mover's OWN color (confirm the screen piece's color doesn't matter for screening purposes, only the captured target's color matters), a Cannon boxed in by adjacent pieces on all sides (it has zero slide destinations, but might still have capture destinations if the adjacent piece plus one more piece beyond line up — construct and verify whichever is actually true for your test position).
- **SOLDIER**: Red soldier at every rank from 0 through 9 (confirming the pre-river/post-river boundary at exactly rank 4→5, and the "far rank has no forward move" edge case at rank 9), same full sweep for a Black soldier (ranks 9 down to 0, boundary at rank 5→4), a Soldier blocked by a friendly piece directly ahead (zero forward destinations once it has sideways options too, or just the one case if pre-river), a Soldier capturing an enemy piece directly ahead.
